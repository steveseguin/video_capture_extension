ℹ️ About VDO.Ninja

🔒 Peer-to-Peer Privacy: Media travels directly between browsers when possible. Encrypted TURN relays may be used when a direct connection is unavailable.

🏠 LAN Optimization: When viewers are on the same local network, video streams directly over LAN without using internet bandwidth.

⚡ Performance Note: Each viewer creates a separate connection. More viewers = more CPU usage on the publisher's device.

💚 Free Service: VDO.Ninja is completely free and open-source. No accounts, no limits on stream duration.

🌍 Network Compatibility: Automatic TURN relays help when a direct connection is blocked by your network.
